How it works

The picks

  • Every team has a win line (e.g. Oklahoma City Thunder – O/U 62.5). Pick Over or Under for all 30 teams.
  • Pick 3 Locks. A Lock is a team you’re extra sure about — it uses your Over/Under pick for that team.
  • Pick the winner of the in-season tournament (the NBA Cup).

The points

  • 1 point for each correct Over/Under.
  • 2 points instead of 1 when a correct pick is also a Lock. A wrong Lock scores 0 — there is no penalty.
  • 3 points for picking the Cup winner.
  • Most points wins; the maximum is 36. Lines end in .5, so there are no pushes. Ties share a rank.

During the season

The standings show two numbers. Locked in counts picks that can no longer change — an Over is locked in once the team passes the line, an Under once the team can’t reach it. On pace adds the open picks that are currently tracking to win — that is, where the team’s projected win total (ESPN’s BPI projection, which weighs the record so far, team strength and the remaining schedule) is on the picked side of the line. When the projection is too close to the line to call, the pick is a toss-up and counts half a point; how close is “too close” shrinks as the season goes on (about two wins in week one, under one win in the last ten games). Projections move with injuries and results, so on-pace picks can flip; only Locked in is final.

Reading the win bars

  • Each bar fills to the team’s current wins, out of 82. The dark tick is the win line, and the numbers underneath count wins in tens. The faint striped stretch ahead of the solid fill is where ESPN projects the team to finish; once a team’s line is settled, the stripes go away.
  • Arrow tags show who picked what, and each one points the way the pick goes: left-pointing tags left of the tick are Unders, right-pointing tags right of the tick are Overs. A small padlock on a tag means that pick is a Lock.
  • Tag colour is how the pick is doing: solid green or red once it’s settled, light green or red while it’s still open and currently on or off pace, and plain white for a toss-up.
  • Tap a name in the key to highlight just that person’s picks.
  • A gold Unanimous stamp on a team means everyone picked the same side. A blue Lone wolf stamp means exactly one person is going against everyone else, and names who. Both need at least three entrants. The “Show” menu narrows the bars to just those teams.
  • “Hide settled teams” removes every team whose line is already decided (it has passed the line, or can no longer reach it), leaving only the teams still in play.
